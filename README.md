# Grandma on the Farm — Recipes from the kitchen of Grandma Harris

A family cookbook made from every card, slip and clipping in Grandma Harris's recipe box — 446 recipes,
transcribed exactly as she wrote them, each printed beside a photograph of the original handwritten card.

**Read it online:** https://grandmaonthefarm.com/

**Download the book (PDF, 346 pages, 79 MB):** see the [latest release](../../releases/latest) —
`Grandma-on-the-Farm.pdf` is the whole book (with covers) for reading; the `print-files` are for sending to a
hardcover printer (US Letter, 8.5 × 11 in: interior PDF + front/back cover panels).

## What's in this repository

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `docs/index.html`, `docs/book.html` | The website and the online edition of the book (small facsimiles) |
| `recipes.json` | Every recipe as structured data — title, who it's from, chapter, ingredients, directions, notes, source photo numbers, raw transcription |
| `print-files/` | Front/back cover PDF (the big interior PDF lives in Releases) |
| `build/` | The generator (`book.py`, `illos.py`, `merged.json`, render scripts). Re-rendering needs the card images, which are not in the repo — see the family copy. |
| `source/transcripts/` | The raw per-photo transcriptions of all 634 photos |

## Printing a hardcover

Upload the interior PDF from the release as a US Letter colour hardcover (346 pages; page 1 is a right-hand
page; even page count). Printers usually want a single wraparound cover sized to their template because the
spine width depends on paper — use the two panels in `print-files/Grandma-on-the-Farm-cover.pdf` as the front
and back, and put *Grandma on the Farm* on the spine.

## About the transcription

Everything is set down as she wrote it — her abbreviations (c̄ = with, H2O, oleo, V.G.), her asides, the sources
she credited. Forty worn or pencilled cards are flagged as harder to read; doubtful words are marked
`[like this?]` and the original card is right there to check. Chapters follow the tab dividers in her box.

Free to share with family and friends. The printed clippings she kept (newspaper and package recipes) are
reproduced for the family's own use.
